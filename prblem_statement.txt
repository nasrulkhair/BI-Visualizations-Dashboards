
1. Solving customer churn
- A fictious dataset about churn from a Telecom provider (Databel)

Task:
- Discover why customers are churning (leaving Databel)


2. Defining churn
- The *churn rate*, also known as the rate of attrition or customer churn, is the rate 
at which customers stop doing business with an entity
- Leaky bucket problem
- Keeping customers is easier than getting new customers.
- Reducing churn is a priority for many companies

3.  Calculating churn

Simplified formula:
- churn rate = customers lost / total number of customers
